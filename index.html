Lesson 1.2: Three Types of AI Intelligence

Standards Alignment Tool for Teachers

Lesson Overview

Lesson 1.2 introduces students to three distinct types of artificial intelligence: Visual AI (computer vision), Auditory AI (speech recognition), and Textual AI (natural language processing). Through real-world examples and analysis of each type's capabilities and limitations, students understand how AI processes different forms of information through pattern recognition. The lesson emphasizes that AI recognizes patterns without truly understanding meaning, preparing students for hands-on work with Alia (a Textual AI) in subsequent lessons.
